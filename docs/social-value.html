social-value

An economic layer for AI agents. Lets an agent hold and move small amounts of value on its own, funded and drained over Lightning. Early release, not production-hardened.

pip install social-value
PyPI huje.tools

Status

social-value is live on PyPI at 0.1.0 as an early, experimental release. It is installable today, but it is not production-hardened. The custody model, backends, and safety work are still maturing, so treat it as a preview and not a dependency you lean on in production.

What it does

It lets an AI agent hold and move small amounts of value on its own, without routing every transfer through an operator's wallet. Lightning is how funds move in and out. The direction of travel is a self-custodial economic identity an agent can manage for itself.
